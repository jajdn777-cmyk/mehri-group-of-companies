Your feelings are subjective. They change daily. Biometric data is objective and stable. It tells you exactly how your body is responding to training. You cannot manage what you do not measure.

![Data analysis](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1460925895917-afdab827c52f?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The subjectivity of exertion
You might feel tired when you are ready to set a personal record. You might feel great when you are on the verge of injury. Biometric markers give you the truth. They remove the guesswork from your training schedule.

## Tracking your metabolic efficiency
Biometrics show you how well you burn fat and use glucose. This knowledge allows you to tailor your nutrition to your specific metabolic needs. You can eat for performance instead of eating for taste.

![Metabolic tracking](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1571019613454-1cb2f99b2d8b?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Integrating data into your routine
Stop following a set program. Start following your own data. If your markers are down, you need more rest. If your markers are up, you need to push harder. This is the only way to reach your full potential.

![Integrated health](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1526628953301-3e589a6a8b74?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

Master your metrics. Master your body. The data does not lie.
