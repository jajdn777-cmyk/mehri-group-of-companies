# Why Your Deep Sleep Is Crashing

You wake up tired. Your brain feels slow. This is a sleep recovery failure. Your deep sleep phase is too short.

![Deep Sleep Recovery](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1541480601022-2308c0f02487?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The Biology of Recovery

Your body heals during deep sleep. Growth hormones release now. Muscle tissue repairs. Brain toxins clear out. If you miss this window, you lose the day.

## The Alcohol Trap

One drink ruins your sleep architecture. It stops the transition to deep cycles. You stay in light sleep. Your heart rate remains high. Stop drinking four hours before bed.

![Sleep Environment](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1511295742364-9119555d73a7?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Temperature Management

Your core temperature must drop. A warm room keeps you awake. Set your thermostat to 18 degrees Celsius. Use breathable sheets. Cold exposure before bed triggers the right signals.

## Fix Your Routine

View sunlight every morning. It sets your internal clock. Avoid blue light after 8 PM. Use a heavy blanket. Consistency is the only path to high performance.
