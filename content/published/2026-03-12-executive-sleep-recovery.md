# Your sleep is failing your career

Success starts in the bedroom. High performance requires cognitive recovery. If you do not recover, you cannot lead.

Lack of sleep reduces decision speed. It clouds your judgment. You become reactive instead of proactive. One night of bad sleep lowers your IQ by several points. You cannot afford this in a competitive environment.
