# The Optimal Zone: Precision Fat Burning

You can burn fat faster. You must watch your heart rate to stay in the right zone. Working harder is not always working smarter.

## Zone 2 Training

Stay in Zone 2 for fat loss. This is 60 to 70 percent of your max heart rate. Your body uses fat for fuel here instead of sugar. It is sustainable and effective.
