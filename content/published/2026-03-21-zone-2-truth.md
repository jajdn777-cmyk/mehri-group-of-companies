# The Truth About Zone 2 Training

You are training too hard. You think high intensity is the only way. You are wrong. Zone 2 training is the foundation of endurance.

![Running Zone 2](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1517836357463-d25dfeac3438?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The Science of Fat Oxidation

Your mitochondria burn fat at a specific heart rate. This is Zone 2. Usually 60 to 70 percent of your maximum. If you go higher, you burn glucose. If you go lower, you lose efficiency.

## The 80-20 Rule

Perform 80 percent of your workouts in Zone 2. Reserve 20 percent for maximum effort. This ratio prevents overtraining. It builds a massive aerobic base.

## Long Term Health

Zone 2 lowers your resting heart rate. It improves blood pressure. It reduces metabolic disease risk. Run slow to get fast. This is the executive standard.
