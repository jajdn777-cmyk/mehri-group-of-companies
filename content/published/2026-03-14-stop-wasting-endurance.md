# STOP WASTING YOUR ENDURANCE TRAINING

Most athletes overtrain the middle zone. You likely run too fast on easy days and too slow on hard days. This mistake stalls progress and increases injury risk.

![Gym Training](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1517836357463-d25dfeac3438?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## POLARIZED TRAINING MODELS

Elite athletes follow a polarized model. They spend eighty percent of time at low intensity. They spend twenty percent at very high intensity. You should avoid the "moderate" zone where fatigue accumulates without maximum benefit.

## MONITORING INTENSITY

You must know your heart rate zones. Use precise tracking to stay within targets. Slow down when your heart rate climbs too high on recovery days. This discipline builds a massive aerobic base.

![Cycling Endurance](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1444492417251-9c84a5fa1c10?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## RECOVERY AS A WEAPON

Endurance improves during rest periods. You build mitochondria when you sleep. Skipping rest days destroys the hard work done in training. You should prioritize sleep as much as your workouts. Consistent tracking helps you see when you are actually recovered.
