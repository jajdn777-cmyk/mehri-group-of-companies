# Your Heart Rate is Lying to You

Generic formulas use age to calculate max heart rate. These formulas are inaccurate for athletes. Individual physiology varies. Your fitness level changes daily. Fatigue affects your heart rate response. Stress alters your baseline. Use personal data to set training zones.

![Running Data](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1523275335684-37898b6baf30?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The Problem with Averages

Averages ignore your unique biology. Training in the wrong zone leads to burnout. High intensity sessions require precision. Low intensity sessions build your aerobic base. Miscalculating these zones wastes effort. Overlapping zones cause plateaus. You need measurements for progress.

## Real Time Feedback

Monitor your heart rate during every workout. Look for trends over weeks. Elevated resting heart rate signals overtraining. Rapid recovery after sprints indicates high fitness. Slow recovery suggests a need for rest. Adjust your intensity based on these signals.
