Brain fog is not a condition. It is a symptom. It indicates systemic inflammation or poor metabolic health. You do not have to live with it.

The first cause is blood sugar instability. Simple carbohydrates cause rapid insulin spikes. This leads to a subsequent crash. Your brain loses its primary energy source. You feel slow. You feel unfocused.

Switch to complex fats. Use medium-chain triglycerides. They provide a steady stream of ketones for the brain. This creates stable energy.

The second cause is dehydration. Your brain is eighty percent water. Even a two percent drop in hydration impairs memory and attention. Drink water with electrolytes immediately upon waking.

The third cause is lack of movement. Stagnation leads to poor circulation. Blood flow to the prefrontal cortex decreases. Take a brisk walk every ninety minutes. This increases oxygen delivery.

Environmental toxins also contribute. Artificial fragrances and poor air quality stress your immune system. Use air filters. Open windows.

Action is required. Remove processed sugars from your diet. Prioritize hydration. Move your body consistently.

Monitor your cognitive state. Notice when you feel sharp. Notice when you feel dull. Identify the variables.

Clarity is power. A focused mind is your greatest asset. Do not let brain fog steal your edge.

Start today. Clear the fog.
