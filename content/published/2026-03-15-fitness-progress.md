Most people fail their fitness goals. They fail because they do not track data. They rely on how they feel. Feelings are deceptive.

You need objective metrics. Weight is a poor indicator of health. It does not distinguish between muscle mass and fat. You need better tools.

Heart rate variability is a critical metric. It measures the variation in time between heartbeats. It indicates the state of your nervous system. A high HRV means you are recovered. A low HRV means you are overtrained.

Resting heart rate is another key marker. A decreasing resting heart rate shows improved cardiovascular efficiency. Check this every morning.

Workout intensity must be measured. Do not just go to the gym. Track your volume. Track your heart rate zones during exercise. Ensure you are pushing into the anaerobic zone for short bursts.
