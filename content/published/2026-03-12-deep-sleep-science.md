# Why You Wake Up Tired Every Morning

You sleep for eight hours. You still feel exhausted. Quantity does not equal quality. Your brain needs deep sleep to repair tissue and clear toxins. Most people lack this critical phase. Sleep is not a passive state. It is an active metabolic process. If you are not hitting your deep sleep targets, you are essentially operating on a low-battery mode every single day.

![Deep Sleep](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1555820585-757443f11d13?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Temperature Control

Your core temperature must drop to initiate sleep. Keep your bedroom cold. Sixty-five degrees is ideal. Use breathable sheets. A hot room keeps your heart rate high. You stay in light sleep all night. This prevents the glymphatic system from flushing out metabolic waste from your brain. If you wake up with a "foggy" feeling, your room is likely too warm. Cooling the body is the fastest way to signal the brain that it is time for deep recovery.

## The Alcohol Trap

You drink a glass of wine to relax. It helps you fall asleep faster. It destroys your sleep architecture. Alcohol blocks REM sleep. It causes frequent micro-awakenings. You do not remember them. Your brain feels the impact the next day. While you might be "out," you are not resting. Your heart rate remains elevated as your liver processes the toxins. This creates a state of physiological stress during the time meant for restoration.
