# The Science of High Performance Sleep

Sleep is your most potent performance enhancer. Quality matters more than quantity. Deep sleep drives cognitive recovery. REM sleep handles emotional processing. You must optimize both to maintain peak output.

![Sleep Recovery](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1511295744337-087fa6723c94?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The Deep Sleep Threshold

Deep sleep occurs in the first half of the night. It is when your body repairs tissue. Growth hormones peak. Your brain clears metabolic waste. If you wake up tired, you likely missed this window. Control your room temperature. Reduce blue light exposure before bed.

## REM Sleep and Creativity

Creative problem solving requires REM sleep. This stage happens more in the later hours of the morning. Cutting your sleep short by two hours can cost you fifty percent of your REM. This leads to irritability. It slows down your processing speed. You cannot afford to lose this advantage.

![Cognitive Focus](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1460925895917-afdab827c52f?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Tracking Sleep Cycles

Measure your sleep stages nightly. Look for consistency in your wake up times. Observe how caffeine and alcohol impact your recovery scores. Data from your biometric sensors will reveal the truth. Your feelings are subjective. The numbers are objective.

![Fitness Tracking](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1544256718-3bcf237f3974?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

Prioritize your sleep to maximize your wake. Performance starts in bed.
