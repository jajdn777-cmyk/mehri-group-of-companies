High pressure environments create a unique physiological load. You must know if your tiredness is mental or physical. Executive stress often masks physical readiness. It makes you feel weak when your muscles are ready to push.

## The Cognitive Cost

Decision making burns glucose. It fatigues the prefrontal cortex. This feels like bodily exhaustion. You might skip a workout when you need it most. Movement is the cure for mental fog.
