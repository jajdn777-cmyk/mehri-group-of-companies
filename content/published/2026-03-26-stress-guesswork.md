# Stop Guessing Your Stress Levels

Stress is a physical reality. It is not just a mental state. High cortisol levels impact your entire body. They degrade sleep quality and metabolic health. Many people believe they are managing stress well. Their physiological data often tells a different story.

## The Stress Feedback Loop

Your nervous system has two primary states. The sympathetic state is for action. The parasympathetic state is for recovery. Chronic stress keeps you in a sympathetic state. This leads to high blood pressure and system inflammation. You cannot manage what you do not measure.

![Physiological Monitoring](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512436991641-6745cdb1723f?auto=format&fit=crop&q=80&w=1000)

## Heart Rate Variability

Heart rate variability measures the time between heartbeats. It is a direct indicator of your autonomic nervous system. High variability suggests a resilient system. Low variability indicates overtraining or chronic stress. Tracking this metric allows you to adjust your load. If your variability is low, push less. If it is high, push more.

## Continuous Monitoring

A snapshot is not enough. You need continuous data to see trends. Wearable technology provides this constant feedback. It tracks how your body responds to specific triggers. You might find that caffeine late in the day spikes your night stress. Or you might see that certain meetings drain your resilience more than others.
