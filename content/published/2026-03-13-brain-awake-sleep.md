# Why your brain stays awake while you sleep

Sleep is not a passive state. Your brain performs critical maintenance during the night. Many professionals sleep for eight hours but wake up tired. This happens because the brain fails to enter deep recovery phases.

![Brain sleep analysis](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1511295742364-91190082d47e?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The stages of cognitive repair

Your sleep cycle consists of four distinct stages. Light sleep occupies the first half of the night. It allows your heart rate to slow down. The real work begins during deep sleep. This is when your brain flushes out metabolic waste. High stress levels keep your brain in a state of hyperarousal. You stay in light sleep longer. Your body rests, but your mind remains active. This lead to brain fog and slow decision making.

## Why temperature control matters

Your core body temperature must drop by two degrees for deep sleep. Warm environments trap heat. This prevents the brain from entering the restorative phase. Many people use heavy blankets or keep their rooms too warm. Cooling your sleeping environment signals your nervous system to shut down. High performers often use cooling pads to maintain an optimal thermal profile. A cool brain is a recovered brain.

## The impact of light on melatonin

Blue light from screens suppresses melatonin production. Melatonin is the hormone that regulates your sleep-wake cycle. Checking emails before bed keeps your cortisol levels high. Cortisol is the enemy of sleep. It tells your brain to stay alert for threats. You must create a digital sunset. Stop using screens sixty minutes before bed. This allows your natural hormones to take over.

## Conclusion

Deep sleep is a skill you can master. Control your environment and your habits. You will see an immediate improvement in your mental clarity. Start tonight by lowering your room temperature. Your brain will thank you in the morning.
