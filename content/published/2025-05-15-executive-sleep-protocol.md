## Optimizing the Sleep Environment
Precision sleep requires a controlled environment. Thermal regulation is paramount; studies suggest that a room temperature of approximately 65°F (18°C) is ideal for facilitating the drop in core body temperature necessary for deep sleep. Furthermore, the elimination of "blue light" exposure at least 90 minutes before sleep is non-negotiable for Melatonin production. High-performance individuals often employ blackout curtains and acoustic optimization to eliminate external stimuli that could trigger micro-awakenings.
