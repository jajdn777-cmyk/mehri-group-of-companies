# Why zone 2 is your secret weapon

Hard work is not always fast work. You build endurance at low intensities. This is zone 2 training.

![Running](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1571019613454-1cb2f99b2d8b?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The science of mitochondrial health

Zone 2 training improves your mitochondria. These are the powerhouses of your cells. When you train at a moderate pace, you use fat for fuel. This increases your metabolic flexibility. You can go longer without hitting the wall.

## Finding your heart rate zones

You cannot guess your intensity. You must measure it. Zone 2 is roughly 60 to 70 percent of your max heart rate. You should be able to hold a conversation. If you are gasping, you are going too fast. Use a reliable tracker to stay in the pocket.

![Monitor](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1510017803434-a899398421b3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Consistency over intensity

Build a base first. Spend eighty percent of your time in zone 2. Spend twenty percent on high intensity. This ratio prevents burnout. It leads to steady progress. You will find that your "slow" pace becomes much faster over time.

![Track](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1533560235473-19e48bc33541?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Build your engine

Start slow to finish strong. Your aerobic base is the foundation of your fitness. Build it with patience and data.

## Summary

Slow down to speed up. Your body will adapt.
