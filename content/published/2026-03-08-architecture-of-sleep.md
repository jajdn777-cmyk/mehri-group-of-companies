Data from the Mehri system can help you pinpoint "thermal spikes" that wake you up or "light leaks" that suppress melatonin production. ## The Role of Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

One of the most critical metrics for assessing recovery is Heart Rate Variability. HRV is a window into your autonomic nervous system. A high HRV indicates that your body is well-rested and ready to take on stress, while a low HRV suggests that you are still in a state of recovery.

By monitoring your HRV trends over time, you can intelligently adjust your training volume. If your HRV is depressed, it might be a day for active recovery rather than a high-intensity session. This proactive approach ensures that you are always training at your maximum capacity without overreaching.
