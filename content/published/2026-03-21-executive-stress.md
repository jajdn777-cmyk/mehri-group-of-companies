# Why Executive Stress Is Killing Your Performance

You are under pressure. High stakes. Long hours. Constant cortisol. Your performance is dropping. Your health is at risk.

![Executive Workload](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512438248247-f0f2a5a8b7f0?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The Cortisol Problem

Cortisol is a stress hormone. It helps in short bursts. In constant levels, it is toxic. It breaks down muscle. It stores fat. It impairs memory.

## Biometric Awareness

You must track your HRV. High heart rate variability means you are recovering. Low HRV means you are burning out. Check your stats daily.

![Tracking Stress](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1507034889033-2c14011ff836?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Breathing As A Tool

Control your breath. Five seconds in. Five seconds out. Do this for five minutes. It triggers the parasympathetic nervous system. Your heart rate will drop. Your focus will return.

## Strategic Rest

Rest is part of the work. Take a 10 minute walk every two hours. Leave your phone. Look at nature. Return with a clear head. This is the path to sustainable leadership.
