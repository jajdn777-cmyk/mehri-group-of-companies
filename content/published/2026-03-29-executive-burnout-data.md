# Why Executive Burnout Ends with Data

Executive burnout is not a personal failure. It is a biological state triggered by chronic stress. You cannot manage what you do not measure. High performance requires precise data.

![Executive Focus](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1499750310107-5fef28a66643?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The Biology of Stress

Your nervous system responds to pressure in predictable ways. Cortisol levels rise. Heart rate variability drops. This shift impacts your decision making. You feel foggy. You lose your edge. Tracking these metrics allows you to pivot before the crash occurs.

## Actionable Recovery Protocols

Recovery must be as intentional as your work. Use the data to time your breaks. Focus on deep sleep cycles. Aim for 90 percent recovery scores before major presentations. Small adjustments in your routine lead to massive gains in cognitive endurance.
