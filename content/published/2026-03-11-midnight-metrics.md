## The 3 AM Diagnostic Window

During deep sleep, your metabolic demands are at their lowest. This is the only time your heart rate reflects your true cardiovascular efficiency. If your 3 AM heart rate is elevated by even 5 beats per minute, something is wrong. It could be late-night digestion. It could be residual alcohol. It could be the early onset of overtraining. Your heart does not lie when the rest of you is unconscious.
