## Decoding the Five Zones
Effective cardiovascular training is divided into five distinct heart rate zones, from Zone 1 (Recovery) to Zone 5 (VO2 Max). While Zone 2 is critical for metabolic flexibility and base endurance, high-intensity intervals in Zone 4 and 5 drive significant aerobic gains. The key is maintaining the correct balance based on your specific performance goals.
