# Why 8 Hours of Sleep is a Myth

Clock hours do not measure biological recovery. Your brain operates in cycles. These cycles last 90 minutes. Interruption of a cycle causes grogginess. You need completed cycles. Quality matters more than total time spent in bed.

![Sleep Recovery](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1517836357463-d25dfeac3438?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## The Truth About Cycles

Deep sleep occurs early in the night. REM sleep dominates later hours. Stages serve functions. Deep sleep repairs tissue. REM sleep processes information. Lack of deep sleep impacts health. Lack of REM sleep affects clarity. Track your stages to understand needs.

## Environmental Factors

Light exposure dictates melatonin production. Blue light from screens delays sleep onset. Keep your room cold. High temperatures disrupt deep sleep cycles. Silence is mandatory for rest. Use blackout curtains to maintain darkness.

## Nutrition and Timing

Caffeine has a long half life. It blocks sleep signals in the brain. Stop consumption early in the day. Alcohol reduces sleep quality. It prevents the brain from entering deep cycles. Large meals before bed increase body temperature. Eat dinner three hours before sleep.

![Calm Environment](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1444464666168-49d633b867ad?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

Establish a consistent wake time. Your body thrives on routine. Consistency stabilizes your circadian rhythm. Focus on recovery scores. Ignore the clock.
