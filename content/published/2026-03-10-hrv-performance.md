# The One Metric That Predicts Your Burnout

You feel fine. You think you can push harder. But your nervous system is screaming. Heart Rate Variability (HRV) is the secret. It measures the time between heartbeats. A high HRV means you are resilient. A low HRV means you are under stress. You must listen to what your heart is telling you.

![Tracking your heart](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1557804483-ef3ae72eba50?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Understanding The Rhythm

HRV is not your heart rate. It is the variation in timing. High variation shows a balanced system. It means you can handle pressure. Low variation shows a dominant sympathetic system. This is the fight or flight mode. If you stay here too long you will crash. You cannot outrun biology.

![Analyzing the data](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1576091160550-2173dba999ef?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Managing Executive Stress

Stress is not the problem. Lack of recovery is the problem. Use HRV to guide your day. If your HRV is low take it easy. Focus on deep work. Avoid high-intensity meetings. If your HRV is high go all out. This is the time to tackle your hardest tasks. You work with your body instead of against it.

## Simple Ways To Improve HRV

Breathe deeply. Focus on long exhales. This activates the vagus nerve. It shifts you back to recovery mode. Eat clean food. Processed sugar kills HRV. Stay hydrated. Dehydration is a physical stressor. These small habits lead to big gains in resilience.

![Focused and ready](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1499750310107-5fef28a66643?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## Conclusion

Data beats intuition. Stop guessing how you feel. Start measuring. When you understand your HRV you control your performance. You avoid the wall. You stay in the game longer.
