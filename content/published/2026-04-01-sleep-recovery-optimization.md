Sleep is the most important part of your training cycle. It is not a break from work. It is the work of recovery. You need deep sleep to consolidate skills and repair tissue. Most people fail to monitor the quality of their rest. They only look at the total duration.

## The Recovery Gap

Short sleep kills your reaction time. It reduces your ability to process glucose. This leads to brain fog and muscle weakness. You cannot train hard if your body is stuck in a state of partial repair.

![Sleep Context](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1534438327276-14e5300c3a48?auto=format&fit=crop&q=80&w=1200)

## Performance Restoration

Active recovery requires data. You must track your heart rate variability during the night. High variability means your nervous system is ready for a heavy load. Low variability suggests a rest day is necessary.

![Gym Recovery](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1491438590914-bc09fcaaf77a?auto=format&fit=crop&q=80&w=1200)

Your bedroom is your performance lab. Control the temperature and light. Use data to find your optimal window for rest. Wake up ready to perform. Restoration is a choice. Make it today.
