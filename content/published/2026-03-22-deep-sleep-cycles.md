# THE TRUTH ABOUT DEEP SLEEP CYCLES

Sleep is not a passive state. It is a highly active period of biological maintenance. Your performance depends on the quality of your deep sleep. You cannot cheat the clock and expect to win. High-performance athletes and executives understand this truth.

![Restful Sleep](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1541781774459-bb2af2f05b55?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## THE BIOLOGY OF PHYSICAL RECONSTRUCTION

Deep sleep occurs early in the night. This is when your body releases growth hormones. Your cells repair their DNA during this phase. Your immune system strengthens its defenses. You need this physical reconstruction to handle high strain. Missing deep sleep leads to physical decline. Your muscles weaken and your recovery slows.

## THE GLYMPHATIC WASTE REMOVAL SYSTEM

Your brain has a waste removal system. It only operates at full capacity during deep sleep. This system flushes out toxic proteins. These proteins build up throughout the day. If you skip deep sleep, these toxins remain. This causes the brain fog you feel the next day. You must clear the waste to maintain cognitive edge.
