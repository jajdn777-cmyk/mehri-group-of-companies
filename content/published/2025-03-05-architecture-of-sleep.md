## The Sleep Cycle Anatomy

Your brain doesn't just "turn off" when you close your eyes. It moves through a complex series of stages: Light Sleep, Deep (Slow Wave) Sleep, and REM (Rapid Eye Movement) Sleep. Each serves a distinct purpose. Deep sleep is when growth hormones are released and tissues are repaired. REM sleep is where cognitive consolidation happens. If you cut your sleep by even two hours, you aren't just tired; you are cognitively impaired.

## Engineering Your Environment

Your bedroom should be a high-performance sanctuary. Circadian rhythm is governed by light, temperature, and timing. Blue light from screens suppresses melatonin—the hormone that signals sleep. Keeping your room temperature at approximately 65°F (18°C) facilitates the drop in core body temperature required for deep sleep. These small environmental "hacks" have an outsized impact on sleep quality.
