# THREE BIOMETRIC DATA POINTS YOU IGNORE

Step counts provide little value. High performance requires deeper analysis. You must look at these three metrics to optimize your health.

![Medical Tech](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1576091160550-2173bdd99611?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## HEART RATE VARIABILITY

Heart rate variability measures your nervous system balance. High variability signals readiness. Low variability indicates stress or fatigue. You should use this data to decide when to train hard.

## OXYGEN SATURATION TRENDS

Nocturnal oxygen levels reveal respiratory health. Drops during sleep signal poor quality. You might suffer from shallow breathing without knowing. Consistent monitoring identifies these patterns early.

![Oxygen Monitoring](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1504813184591-01592fd039e3?auto=format&fit=crop&w=1200&q=80)

## BODY COMPOSITION CHANGES

Weight does not tell the full story. Muscle mass and body fat percentages matter more. You should track 3D biometric changes over time. This approach ensures your diet works as intended.
